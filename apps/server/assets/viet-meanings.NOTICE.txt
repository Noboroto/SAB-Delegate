viet-meanings.tsv: word <TAB> source <TAB> meaning, one Vietnamese meaning per word of
viet-words.txt that has one. Rebuilt with a one-off script from:

- WIKTIONARY: the first Vietnamese definition of the word in the Vietnamese Wiktionary dump
  (https://dumps.wikimedia.org/viwiktionary/), wiki markup removed. Wiktionary text is
  licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); this file is shared under the same terms.
- VNTK: the first definition in dictionary.json of https://github.com/vntk/dictionary,
  used for words Wiktionary has no definition for. The repository is published under the
  MIT licence; its definitions may derive from an earlier Vietnamese dictionary.
